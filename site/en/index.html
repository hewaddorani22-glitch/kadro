How it works

  1. 1

    Log the meal

    Photo, a short description or a barcode. Whatever is fastest for you right now.

  2. 2

    Confirm the ingredients

    You see what was detected and fix the portion and the ingredients with one tap.

  3. 3

    The day sets itself up again

    Calories and protein for the rest of the day are recalculated — with three concrete suggestions for home, the shop or eating out.

Honest numbers, not false precision

Nutrition values from a photo are estimates, and Kandro says so. Values carry a tilde and a confidence level, uncertain ingredients are flagged, and you correct every portion before anything is saved.

Where the numbers come from: for typical German dishes Kandro uses reviewed reference values from the Bundeslebensmittelschlüssel 4.0 of the Max Rubner-Institut. Individual ingredients are matched against USDA FoodData Central, packaged products through Open Food Facts.

What a photo fundamentally cannot see is oil, butter and sauce. That is exactly why correcting a portion is one tap and not a hidden setting.

Your data stays yours

  • The original photo is discarded after the analysis and never stored.
  • No account needed to start. You only need an email once you want to secure your history.
  • You delete your account and all data directly in the app, without emailing anyone.
  • Anonymous usage analytics are off by default.

What Kandro is not

Kandro is a general wellness and planning tool. It does not diagnose, does not treat any condition and does not replace medical or dietetic advice. If you have health complaints, are pregnant, have an eating disorder or follow a medically prescribed diet, please seek qualified advice.
